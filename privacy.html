Privacy Policy

Purpose · effective October 5, 2026

Purpose is an iPhone app for semen retention, published by the developer shown on its App Store page (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the app and its widget. You can reach us at purpose.retention.app@gmail.com.

  • Everything you put into Purpose stays on your iPhone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive your streak, your journal or anything you write.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Apple. Purpose checks with Apple, on your iPhone, which plan your Apple Account owns; we receive nothing about you or how you paid.

What Purpose keeps on your iPhone

  • Your streak: when it started, and your past streaks with when each ended and the kind of relapse you chose.
  • Wet dreams you log (the time only).
  • Your journal: each day’s mood, energy and what you write.
  • Your urge log: when an urge passed, the way you used, what set it off and any note.
  • Your answers from the start: what you want most and your own words, your goal, how often you released before, what triggers your urges and where you are on the path.
  • Your settings, such as your reminders and their time, and small markers such as when you last made a backup.

Some of this is about your sex life, which the law treats as especially sensitive. It never leaves your iPhone unless you send it yourself in a backup, as described below.

Purpose works out the sun and the moon from your iPhone’s time zone. It does not use your location.

How it is used

Only to run Purpose for you: your streak and its lights, your journal and profile, the help when an urge comes, and the reminders you turn on. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • In Purpose’s own storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. Your records are locked away while your iPhone is locked.
  • The widget receives only when your streak started, your best streak, whether Purpose is yours and until when, and the place Purpose estimates from your time zone for the sky, to show them on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. If Purpose isn’t yours, it receives no streak at all.
  • Like your other apps’ data, Purpose’s data is included in your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), which Apple or your computer keep under their own terms. Purpose has no sync of its own.
  • If Purpose ever finds its storage damaged, it sets the damaged copy aside on your iPhone instead of deleting it. It goes when you delete the app.

Backups you make

When you choose Back up, Purpose locks everything that is yours in a file with a password you choose, then hands the file to your iPhone’s share sheet, and you decide where it goes. Purpose never stores the password, and it cannot be recovered: without it, nobody can open the file, us included. The file’s name shows the day it was made. Restoring reads only a file you pick.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Apple. Buying, Restore purchases and Manage subscription go through the App Store. Purpose asks Apple which plan your Apple Account owns and, for the monthly plan, when it renews or ends and at what price. Apple handles your payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.
  • Our web host. Terms of Use and this page open in a browser inside the app. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, receives your device’s network address and browser details when a page loads. These pages use no cookies or analytics; the home page only remembers in your browser, until you close the tab, that its opening has played.
  • Crash reports, if you allow them. If you turn on Share With App Developers in your iPhone’s Settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us that don’t identify you. You can turn it off there at any time.

Apple and our web host handle this under their own privacy policies, which protect it at least as well as this one does. Reminders and alerts are set up on your iPhone itself; none go through a server.

No tracking, no selling

Purpose contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It uses no advertising identifier and does not follow you across other apps or websites. We don’t sell or share personal data.

Your choices

  • Notifications: Purpose asks before sending any. You can turn each kind off in the profile, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Settings → Notifications → Purpose.
  • Your answers: change them in the profile.
  • Your journal: delete a past entry by swiping it.
  • Back up and restore from the profile.
  • Delete everything: delete the app, and iOS removes all its data from your iPhone. Copies stay in iPhone backups made before and in backup files you saved, until you delete those too.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete; if you write to us, we will tell you how to do it on your iPhone. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Lithuania, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt), or the one where you live.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it once we no longer need it to help you.

How long it is kept

On your iPhone, until you delete it or the app. Backup files you saved, until you delete them.

Children

Purpose is for adults, 18 and over. It isn’t meant for children, and because it collects nothing, we hold no data about anyone, children included.
